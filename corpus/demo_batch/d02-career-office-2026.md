# Margalla Institute of Technology: Career Services Office Guide (2026)

## 1. Internships

1.1 A student of the sixth semester or higher may apply for an internship through the career services office. The internship lasts at least 8 weeks.

1.2 A student must submit the internship report within 2 weeks of the last day of the internship.

## 2. Job fair

2.1 The annual job fair is held in the first week of March in the main auditorium. Final-year students register for it at the career services office.

2.2 A student must bring three printed copies of the CV and the student card to the job fair.

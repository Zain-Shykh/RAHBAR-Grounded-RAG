# Margalla Institute of Technology: Hostel Residence Rules (2026)

## 1. Entry and visitors

1.1 Residents must return to the hostel before 11:00 pm. A resident who returns later must be signed in by the warden on duty.

1.2 Visitors are received in the common room between 4:00 pm and 7:00 pm. A visitor may not enter the rooms.

## 2. Rooms and fees

2.1 The hostel fee is Rs 45,000 per semester and is paid with the tuition fee. A refundable security deposit of Rs 10,000 is taken at the first admission.

2.2 Cooking appliances and heaters are not allowed in the rooms. A room is inspected once a month.

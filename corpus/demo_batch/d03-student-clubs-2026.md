# Margalla Institute of Technology: Student Societies Handbook (2026)

## 1. Forming a society

1.1 A new society needs at least 15 founding members and one faculty advisor. The proposal is submitted to the student affairs office.

1.2 A society that does not hold an event for two consecutive semesters is deregistered.

## 2. Funding

2.1 A registered society may request up to Rs 25,000 per semester for events. The request is made at least 3 weeks before the event.

2.2 Unused funds are returned to the student affairs office at the end of the semester.
